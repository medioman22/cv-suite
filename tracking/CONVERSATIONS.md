# 💬 Conversations — analysis & suggested replies

Threads (LinkedIn DMs, or any channel you can't auto-send through) that need a
human to paste-and-send a reply. One entry per thread: who they are, where it
stalled, and a ready-to-send reply. The UI **Conversations** page
(`python3 ui/build_conversations.py`, or served live at `/conversations.html`)
lists these; the reply pops up on hover — copy it, tweak it, paste it into the
actual thread. Nothing here sends automatically.

Tone: warm, confident, specific, forward-moving. Always propose a concrete next step.

**Priority tags** (used in the `### Name · Company — <tags>` heading, any combo):
🎯 domain bullseye · 🇨🇭 local / no-relocation (retune to your own region) ·
🌍 strong but relocation · 💬 keep-warm / low priority.
